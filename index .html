Stop guessing what to stock

Many shopkeepers decide what to reorder from memory. Popular items run out, and slow items sit on the shelf and lock up money. Trendora reads the sales and stock data you already have and shows you what is really happening.

  1. Upload your fileExport your sales and stock data as CSV or Excel from your shop, Instagram, WhatsApp, website or marketplace.
  2. We check and calculateTrendora checks the file for errors and works out your key numbers.
  3. You see what to doGet dashboards, stock risks and simple reorder suggestions.

What is inside Trendora

  • Sales dashboardRevenue, orders, average order value and trends over time.
  • Product insightsBest sellers and slow movers. Size, colour and design demand where your data has it.
  • Customer insightsSee new customers and repeat customers.
  • Stock risk alertsLow stock, items at risk and slow-moving stock.
  • Demand estimatesSimple, transparent forecasts. They are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Reorder suggestionsPlain advice, such as: this shoe sells fast and stock is low, so review your reorder.

Made for small and medium retailers who sell through a shop, Instagram, WhatsApp, a website or a marketplace. If you can export your sales to CSV or Excel, Trendora can read it.

What Trendora is not

Trendora analyses the data you upload. It is not billing or POS software, it is not a marketplace, and it does not manage orders, listings or stock on other platforms. Live connections to other tools are a future idea and are not available today.
